Add Aumazing to your Home Screen

Open this page in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen and Add. Keep Open as Web App on if shown.

iPhone and iPad

Install Aumazing on iPhone

First, open the Aumazing link in Safari.

  1. Tap ShareTap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Tap Add to Home ScreenScroll through the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap AddKeep the name Aumazing, then tap Add.
  4. Open AumazingTap the Aumazing icon on your Home Screen.

ⓘ If Open as Web App appears, keep it turned on.
